<h2 id="about-me" style="padding-top:0px;">About Me</h2>

I am currently a final-year Ph.D. student at <a href="https://github.com/thuml" target="_blank">Machine Learning Group</a> (THUML), <a href="https://www.thss.tsinghua.edu.cn/" target="_blank">School of Software</a>, <a href="https://www.tsinghua.edu.cn/en/" target="_blank">Tsinghua University</a>, advised by Prof. <a href="http://ise.thss.tsinghua.edu.cn/~mlong/" target="_blank">Mingsheng Long</a>. Before that, I received my Bachelor's degrees in Software Engineering, and Pure and Applied Mathematics (second degree), from <a href="https://www.tsinghua.edu.cn/en/" target="_blank">Tsinghua University</a>. In high school, I was quite into <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Competitive_programming" target="_blank">competitive programming</a> and used to compete in <a href="https://www.noi.cn/" target="_blank">Olympiad in Informatics</a> (OI).

I am also the co-founder and chief research officer at [MirroS](https://mirros.ai), where we are on a mission to build **physical recursive self-improvement (RSI)**. Previously, I interned at [ByteDance Seed](https://seed.bytedance.com/zh/).

My research goal is to develop fundamental techniques of **general autonomous intelligence**, especially **world models**, and advance their applications in **physical AI**. 

Currently, my research interests include:

- **World abstraction:** How can world models learn abstract, compact, and generalizable representations beyond raw observations?
- **Agentic physical reasoning:** How can agents combine world models, prior knowledge, and simulation to reason and act in complex physical environments?
- **Learning from experience:** How can agents continuously update their world understanding through interaction and acquire new capabilities from experience?
